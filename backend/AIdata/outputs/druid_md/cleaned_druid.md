CLASS
druid

CORE TRAITS
Primary Ability: Wisdom
Hit Die: d8
Saving Throws: intelligence, wisdom
Skill Proficiencies (choose 2): arcana, animal handling, insight, medicine, nature, perception, religion, survival
Weapon Proficiencies: simple weapons
Armor Training: light armor, shields
Tool Proficiencies: Herbalism Kit
Starting Equipment:
  Option A: Leather Armor, Shield, Sickle, Druidic Focus (Quarterstaff), Explorer's Pack, Herbalism Kit, 9 GP
  Option B: 30 GP
Multiclass Gains: hit die d8, light armor and shields, druid level 1 features

FEATURES BY LEVEL
Level 1 — Spellcasting
Action Type: none
Uses: none
Recharge: none
Text: You can cast Druid spells. You know two cantrips from the Druid spell list and learn additional cantrips at Druid levels 4 and 10. You regain expended spell slots on a Long Rest. You prepare level 1+ Druid spells (initially choose four 1st-level Druid spells); the number of spells you can prepare increases with Druid level per the Druid Features table; you can change your prepared spells after finishing a Long Rest. Wisdom is your spellcasting ability. You can use a Druidic Focus as a spellcasting focus.

Level 1 — Druidic
Action Type: none
Uses: none
Recharge: none
Text: You know Druidic. You always have the Speak with Animals spell prepared. You can leave hidden Druidic messages that are automatically spotted by creatures who know Druidic; others detect a message's presence with a DC 15 Intelligence (Investigation) check but can't decipher it without magic.

Level 1 — Primal Order
Action Type: none
Uses: none
Recharge: none
Text: Choose Magician or Warden. Magician: you know one extra cantrip from the Druid spell list and gain a bonus to Intelligence (Arcana or Nature) checks equal to your Wisdom modifier (minimum +1). Warden: gain proficiency with martial weapons and medium armor.

Level 2 — Wild Shape
Action Type: bonus action
Uses: 2 (gains additional uses at higher levels)
Recharge: short or long rest
Text: As a bonus action, you assume a Beast form you know and stay in that form for a number of hours equal to half your Druid level or until you use Wild Shape again, are Incapacitated, or die; you can end the form early as a bonus action. You know a limited number of Beast forms (see Beast Shapes table) chosen from Beast stat blocks with a specified maximum CR and restrictions on fly speed; you can replace one known form after finishing a Long Rest. When you assume a form you gain Temporary Hit Points equal to your Druid level. Your game statistics are replaced by the Beast's stat block except you retain your creature type; Hit Points; Hit Point Dice; Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma scores; class features; languages; and feats; you retain your skill and saving throw proficiencies and use your proficiency bonus for them, and if a Beast's skill or saving throw modifier is higher you use the Beast's. You can't cast spells while in Beast form, but shape-shifting doesn't end Concentration on existing spells. Handling objects is determined by the form's limbs; you choose whether equipment falls, merges with, or is worn by the form; worn equipment functions as normal subject to DM adjudication; merged equipment has no effect while in that form.

Level 5 — Wild Resurgence
Action Type: none
Uses: per turn (first effect); fixed 1 (second effect)
Recharge: per turn; long rest
Text: Once on each of your turns, if you have no uses of Wild Shape left, you can give yourself one use by expending a spell slot (no action required). In addition, you can expend one use of Wild Shape (no action required) to create a level 1 spell slot for yourself; you can't use this second option again until you finish a Long Rest.

Level 7 — Elemental Fury
Action Type: none
Uses: none (Potent Spellcasting); once per turn (Primal Strike)
Recharge: none
Text: Choose one option. Potent Spellcasting: add your Wisdom modifier to the damage you deal with any Druid cantrip. Primal Strike: once on each of your turns when you hit with an attack using a weapon or a Beast form's attack, you can deal an extra 1d8 Cold, Fire, Lightning, or Thunder damage (choose when you hit).

Level 15 — Improved Elemental Fury
Action Type: none
Uses: none
Recharge: none
Text: Improves the Elemental Fury option you chose. Potent Spellcasting: when you cast a Druid cantrip with a range of 10 feet or greater, that spell's range increases by 300 feet. Primal Strike: the extra damage increases to 2d8.

Level 18 — Beast Spells
Action Type: none
Uses: none
Recharge: none
Text: While in Wild Shape, you can cast spells in Beast form except for any spell that has a Material component with a specified cost or that consumes its Material component.

Level 19 — Epic Boon
Action Type: none
Uses: none
Recharge: none
Text: Gain an Epic Boon feat or another feat of your choice for which you qualify.

Level 20 — Archdruid
Action Type: none
Uses: none
Recharge: long rest (for Nature Magician)
Text: Evergreen Wild Shape: whenever you roll initiative and have no uses of Wild Shape left, you regain one expended use. Nature Magician: you can convert a number of unexpended uses of Wild Shape into a single spell slot, with each use contributing 2 spell levels; once you use this benefit, you can't do so again until you finish a Long Rest. Longevity: your aging rate is reduced (for every ten years that pass, your body ages only one year).

SUBCLASSES
Circle of the Land
Level 3 — Circle of the Land Spells
Action Type: none
Uses: none
Recharge: none
Text: Whenever you finish a Long Rest, choose one land type: arid, polar, temperate, or tropical. You have the spells listed for your chosen land type prepared for your Druid level and lower.

Granted Spells by Level:
  Arid: L3=Blur, Burning Hands, Fire Bolt; L5=Fireball; L7=Blight; L9=Wall of Stone
  Polar: L3=Fog Cloud, Hold Person, Ray of Frost; L5=Sleet Storm; L7=Ice Storm; L9=Cone of Cold
  Temperate: L3=Misty Step, Shocking Grasp, Sleep; L5=Lightning Bolt; L7=Freedom of Movement; L9=Tree Stride
  Tropical: L3=Acid Splash, Ray of Sickness, Web; L5=Stinking Cloud; L7=Polymorph; L9=Insect Plague

Level 3 — Land's Aid
Action Type: magic action
Uses: expend a use of Wild Shape
Recharge: none
Text: As a magic action, expend a use of Wild Shape and choose a point within 60 feet; create a 10-foot-radius sphere centered on that point. Each creature of your choice in the sphere must make a Constitution saving throw against your spell save DC, taking 2d6 Necrotic damage on a failed save or half on success; one creature of your choice in the area regains 2d6 Hit Points. Damage and healing increase by 1d6 at Druid levels 10 (3d6) and 14 (4d6).

Level 6 — Natural Recovery
Action Type: none
Uses: once per long rest (for each effect)
Recharge: long rest
Text: You can cast one of the level 1+ spells you have prepared from your Circle Spells feature without expending a spell slot once per Long Rest. In addition, as part of finishing a Short Rest you can recover expended spell slots with a combined level equal to or less than half your Druid level (round up), excluding level 6+ slots; once you recover slots this way, you can't do so again until you finish a Long Rest.

Level 10 — Nature's Ward
Action Type: none
Uses: none
Recharge: none
Text: You are immune to the Poisoned condition. You have Resistance to a damage type associated with your chosen land type (see Nature's Ward table).

Tables:
  Nature's Ward | Land Type ; Resistance -> Arid ; Fire ; Temperate ; Lightning ; Polar ; Cold ; Tropical ; Poison

Level 14 — Nature's Sanctuary
Action Type: magic action (manifest); bonus action (move)
Uses: expend a use of Wild Shape
Recharge: none
Text: As a magic action, expend a use of Wild Shape to create spectral trees and vines in a 15-foot cube on the ground within 120 feet; they last 1 minute or until you are Incapacitated or die. You and your allies have Half Cover while in that area and your allies gain the current Resistance from Nature's Ward while there. As a bonus action, you can move the cube up to 60 feet to a point on the ground within 120 feet.

Circle of the Moon
Level 3 — Circle Forms
Action Type: none
Uses: none
Recharge: none
Text: When you assume a Wild Shape form, the maximum Challenge Rating of the form equals your Druid level divided by 3 (round down). Until you leave the form, your AC equals 13 + your Wisdom modifier if that total is higher than the Beast's AC. You gain Temporary Hit Points equal to three times your Druid level when you assume the form.

Level 3 — Circle of the Moon Spells

Granted Spells by Level:

  L5: Conjure Animals
  L7: Fount of Moonlight
  L9: Mass Cure Wounds

Level 6 — Improved Circle Forms
Action Type: none
Uses: none
Recharge: none
Text: While in Wild Shape, Lunar Radiance: each attack in Wild Shape can deal either its normal damage type or Radiant damage, chosen each time you hit. Increased Toughness: add your Wisdom modifier to Constitution saving throws.

Level 10 — Moonlight Step
Action Type: bonus action
Uses: scales with Wisdom modifier (minimum 1)
Recharge: long rest (can expend a level 2+ spell slot per use to regain)
Text: As a bonus action, teleport up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space you can see and have Advantage on your next attack roll before the end of the turn. You can use this feature a number of times equal to your Wisdom modifier (minimum once); you regain all expended uses on a Long Rest and can also regain uses by expending a level 2+ spell slot for each use restored (no action required).

Level 14 — Lunar Form
Action Type: none
Uses: none
Recharge: none
Text: Improved Lunar Radiance: once per turn, when you hit with a Wild Shape attack you can deal an extra 2d10 Radiant damage. Shared Moonlight: when you use Moonlight Step you can also teleport one willing creature within 10 feet of you to an unoccupied space within 10 feet of your destination.

Level 3 — Wrath of the Sea
Action Type: bonus action
Uses: expend a use of Wild Shape
Recharge: none
Text: As a bonus action, expend a use of Wild Shape to manifest a 5-foot Emanation of ocean spray around you for 10 minutes; it ends early if you dismiss it, manifest it again, or become Incapacitated. When manifested and as a bonus action on subsequent turns, you can choose a creature in the Emanation; the target must make a Constitution saving throw against your spell save DC or take Cold damage and, if Large or smaller, be pushed up to 15 feet away. The damage is a number of d6 equal to your Wisdom modifier (minimum 1).

Level 6 — Aquatic Affinity
Action Type: none
Uses: none
Recharge: none
Text: The Emanation from Wrath of the Sea increases to a 10-foot radius. You gain a Swim Speed equal to your Speed.

Level 10 — Stormborn
Action Type: none
Uses: none
Recharge: none
Text: While your Wrath of the Sea Emanation is active, you gain a Fly Speed equal to your Speed and Resistance to Cold, Lightning, and Thunder damage.

Level 14 — Oceanic Gift
Action Type: bonus action
Uses: expend one or two Wild Shape uses
Recharge: none
Text: When manifesting the Wrath of the Sea Emanation, you can manifest it around one willing creature within 60 feet instead of yourself; that creature uses your spell save DC and Wisdom modifier for the Emanation. You can manifest the Emanation around both that creature and yourself if you expend two uses of Wild Shape instead of one.

Circle of the Stars
Level 3 — Star Map
Action Type: none
Uses: cast Guiding Bolt without spell slot a number equal to Wisdom modifier (minimum 1)
Recharge: long rest
Text: You possess a Tiny star chart that functions as a Spellcasting Focus. While holding the map you have Guidance and Guiding Bolt prepared and can cast Guiding Bolt without expending a spell slot a number of times equal to your Wisdom modifier (minimum once); you regain expended uses on a Long Rest. If you lose the map, you can perform a 1-hour ceremony during a Short or Long Rest to create a replacement; this ceremony destroys the previous map.

Level 3 — Starry Form
Action Type: bonus action
Uses: expend a use of Wild Shape
Recharge: none
Text: As a bonus action, expend a use of Wild Shape to assume a Starry Form for up to 10 minutes; you retain your game statistics. The form sheds Bright Light in a 10-foot radius and Dim Light for an additional 10 feet and ends early if you dismiss it, become Incapacitated, or use the feature again. When you assume the form choose one constellation: Archer, Chalice, or Dragon. Archer: when you activate the form and as a bonus action on subsequent turns, you can make a ranged spell attack against one creature within 60 feet that deals Radiant damage equal to 1d8 + your Wisdom modifier on a hit. Chalice: whenever you cast a spell using a spell slot that restores Hit Points to a creature, you or another creature within 30 feet regains Hit Points equal to 1d8 + your Wisdom modifier. Dragon: when you make an Intelligence or Wisdom check or a Constitution saving throw to maintain Concentration, you can treat a roll of 9 or lower on the d20 as a 10.

Level 6 — Cosmic Omen
Action Type: reaction
Uses: scales with Wisdom modifier (minimum 1)
Recharge: long rest
Text: After finishing a Long Rest, consult your Star Map and roll a die; until your next Long Rest you gain a special Reaction based on even or odd. Weal (even): as a Reaction, when a creature you can see within 30 feet is about to make a D20 test, roll 1d6 and add it to that total. Woe (odd): as a Reaction, roll 1d6 and subtract it from the total. You can use this Reaction a number of times equal to your Wisdom modifier (minimum once); you regain expended uses on a Long Rest.

Level 10 — Twinkling Constellations
Action Type: none
Uses: none
Recharge: none
Text: In your Starry Form, Archer and Chalice effects increase from 1d8 to 2d8; while Dragon is active you gain a Fly Speed of 20 feet and can hover. At the start of each of your turns while in Starry Form, you can change which constellation glimmers.

Level 14 — Full of Stars
Action Type: none
Uses: none
Recharge: none
Text: While in Starry Form you gain Resistance to Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing damage.
